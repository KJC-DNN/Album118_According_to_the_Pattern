# Album 118 — According to the Pattern
The Pattern Shown

The Holy Tent and Solomon’s Temple—the earthly sanctuaries made so God could dwell among His people.

# Track list:
1. Make Me a Sanctuary (Narrative / Teaching – Exodus 25:8)
2. The Courtyard and the Altar (Poetic)
3. Holy Place Furnishings (Teaching)
4. Jachin and Boaz (Narrative of the Temple pillars)
5. From Tent to House (Spoken Story – David’s plan, Solomon’s work)
6. Glory Filled the House (Praise)
7. A Copy and Shadow (Teaching – Hebrews 8:5)
8. God Among His People (Praising)
   
# Study note: 
Both sanctuaries follow the pattern given by God; both teach graded holiness and mediated access.

# Prayer note: 
Lord, You desire to dwell among Your people. Teach us the holiness Your sanctuaries displayed.
